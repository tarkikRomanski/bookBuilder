# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Avid readers, book lovers, and literary enthusiasts managing personal book collections and reading journeys. Their context is tracking active reading, reflecting on progress, and organizing digital book catalogs efficiently without distractions.

## Product Purpose
BookBuilder provides a seamless digital bookshelf and literary progress tracker. It enables users to browse library collections, track page/chapter completion, view book details, and maintain an organized reading catalog. Success means effortless visual progress tracking, fast catalog browsing, and an immersive literary environment.

## Positioning
A minimalist, distraction-free digital shelf for reading management.

## Brand Personality
Refined, literary, focused. Tone is quiet, authoritative, and elegant. Evokes the atmosphere of a curated dark-mode editorial studio with warm amber/gold accents and glass card surfaces.

## Anti-references
Generic corporate SaaS dashboards with dense spreadsheets, harsh 1px borders, and plain blue accents. Avoid over-cluttered social feeds, neon gradients, and low-contrast light-gray text on tinted white backgrounds.

## Design Principles
- **Atmospheric Focus**: Prioritize dark, quiet surfaces that keep the reader's attention centered on literary content and cover art.
- **Visual Progress Clarity**: Present reading completion metrics through clear, tactile visual indicators rather than plain tables or unstyled numbers.
- **Editorial Typography**: Balance expressive serif headlines with legible, high-contrast sans-serif UI elements.
- **Restrained Motion**: Use subtle transitions and micro-interactions that feel purposeful and never distract from reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Enforce strict contrast ratios (≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3:1 for large display elements).
- Full keyboard navigation and visible focus rings across all interactive elements.
- Accessible ARIA labeling for custom interactive components and modals.
- Respect `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` across all animated UI elements.
